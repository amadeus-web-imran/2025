### The Most Famous One

While many artists have made Pietàs, when people say _"The Pietà,"_ they almost always mean the one **Michelangelo** carved when he was just **24 years old**.

| Feature | Why it’s special |
| --- | --- |
| **The Marble** | It’s carved from a single, solid block of white Carrara marble. It looks so soft you’d think you could dent it with your finger. |
